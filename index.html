Our Story

About Nextexture

Nextexture started with a simple problem: most styling products leave hair stiff, greasy or flat by lunchtime. We wanted something light that adds real volume and grip, and still lets you restyle through the day.

Simple, effective formulas

Our texture powders give lift at the roots and a dry matte finish, without the weight or residue of waxes and pastes.

We are a small team based in New Zealand, and every order is packed and sent by us. If you have a question about which powder suits your hair, get in touch.
